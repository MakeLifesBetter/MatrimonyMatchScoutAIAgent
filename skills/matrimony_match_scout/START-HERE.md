# Start here — please read this first (2 minutes)

> ⬅️ **Main page & installation steps:** [README](../../README.md)

## What this does for you
You have too many profiles and chats on your matrimony account to check one by
one. The Scout reads them for you, compares each profile with what YOU said you
want, and shows you a short list of the best matches, with the reason for each
one — and what is risky or not known yet.

Next time, it shows you only what is new. It never shows you the same profile
twice. If someone's profile changes, they come back once, marked **Updated**,
with what changed.

## How long it takes — and why you can walk away
Muse reads each profile carefully, one at a time, like a person would. That
takes about **30–60 seconds per profile**. So each run reads **5 profiles**
(about 5–10 minutes) unless you ask for more — up to **10 per run** (about
8–15 minutes). Want more than 10? Just say so, for example "read 20 this time".
Muse tells you the estimate before it starts and the time left as it goes.
So: answer the questions, log in, check one profile — then **leave or close the
app and come back later**. Your shortlist will be waiting.

## The only 3 things you will ever be asked to do
1. **Answer 5 short questions** — which site, who the search is for, the age
   range, the places that are okay, and one dealbreaker. Change any answer
   later just by saying so, like "change my age range to 26 to 30".
2. **Log in to your matrimony site yourself**, in the browser window that
   opens. You type your own password. The Scout never sees it and never stores
   it. Use mobile number + OTP or email + password — Google blocks
   "Sign in with Google" in assistant-controlled browsers.
3. **Check one profile.** Before reading everything, the Scout shows you what
   it read from ONE profile. Say "yes", or correct it ("age is 28").

Everything else happens on its own, slowly, like a person would read. It only
reads — nothing is sent, accepted or rejected on the site. Say "stop" at any
time; progress is saved after every profile, so "carry on" picks up from there.

## What you get
A short summary in the chat, in three parts (made-up people):

> **Summary:** 8 profiles in this report - 7 new, 1 updated; 2 left out by your
> dealbreakers. 3 match your interests well, 3 need your attention.
>
> **Match your interests:**
> 1. Asha, 27, Seattle - Software Engineer. Score 90/100 (confidence high).
>    Why it fits: Lives in Seattle, one of your places; Education (MS) meets your minimum
> 2. Priya, 28, Bellevue - Data Analyst. Score 85/100 ...
> 3. Divya, 29, San Jose - Product Manager. Score 75/100 ...
>
> **Need your attention:**
> - Asha: waiting for your reply (asked about a phone or video call)
> - Meera: changed since you last saw it - profession: Analyst -> Manager
> - Lakshmi: not shown, please check - education, smoking

…with an **Excel sheet** (Ranked Matches / Profile Details / Updated Since Last
Report) and the full report attached right there in the chat. Then Muse asks:
**"Want me to send this to WhatsApp?"** Say yes, connect WhatsApp once if Muse
asks, pick who it goes to (yourself or family), check the message — and it is
sent only when you say "send".

## Talk in your own language
You can talk to Muse in **your own language** — Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada,
Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Urdu, English, or a mix like
Hinglish. Muse answers in the same language, and it can read profiles, chats and
call notes in other languages too. (The attached Excel sheet keeps English
column names.)

## Things you can say
"run matrimony scout" · "read 10 this time" · "read the next 5" · "how much is left?" ·
"give me the report" · "check status" · "how do you read my data?" ·
"change my age range to 26 to 30" · "add Pune to my places" ·
"my dealbreaker is smoking" · "pass on 2" · "interested in Asha" ·
"why is 1 ranked first" · "draft a reply to Asha" · "send it to WhatsApp" ·
"what did you show me before" · "read my files" · "refine my criteria"

There are no commands to learn — say it your way.

## After a call (optional)
Tell Muse how a call went, in your own words, and it adds a short call summary
with green and red flags to that person. You can also send a recording — **but
only if the other person agreed to be recorded.** Recording a call without
everyone's consent is illegal in many places, including California. Muse keeps
only the short summary and deletes the recording.

## What stays private
- The Scout keeps its notes in its own private folder in your Muse workspace.
  Nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere by the Scout.
- Your report comes to you in the chat. Who you forward it to is up to you —
  Muse only sends something for you after you approve the exact message.
- Chats are kept as short notes ("they asked about a call this weekend"), never
  copies of your conversations.
- No face recognition and no judging of photos, ever.
- Say **"delete all my scout data"** and, once you confirm, everything is gone.

## Try it with zero risk first
In Muse, say **"show me a matrimony scout demo"** — it runs on made-up profiles,
so you see exactly what you will get before any login.
